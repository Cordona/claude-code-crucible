# The spec

How SKILL.md's The spec settles an effort's spec; read when SKILL.md step 4 runs it before v1, step 7 runs it after a grill, a ticket read after v1 calls for a revised or new spec, Resume finds an open spec talk, or a flow or the human's own ask runs it alone after v1. The approved spec and a recorded skip are as SKILL.md's The spec names them; "extend" is SKILL.md step 5. **Record the outcome:** before v1, it joins v1's entries (SKILL.md step 4), and no version is written here; after v1, extend.

`<crucible.sh>` is `"${CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR:-$HOME/.claude}/skills/crucible/scripts/crucible.sh"`; run its `--help` once, before its first use in a session.

**The spec's gates.** Its offer and its approval are gates, never talk, and none opens while another gate waits. Each is shown through `procedure-approval-gate` as SKILL.md step 7 shows "what next?", with these inputs instead:
- **The renderer:** `sh "${CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR:-$HOME/.claude}/skills/procedure-context/scripts/render-spec-gate.sh" --plan-file <the plan file>`; its `--help` is its contract.
- **The gate's label:** `spec-offer` or `spec-approval`, as step 3 or 4 sets, with `--effort '<effort folder>'`: `context.sh` looks for the gate's record there.
- **On "now ask":** run the renderer with `--plan-file <the plan file> --question`, and copy its `header`, `question` and `options` into one AskUserQuestion as they are.
- **The answer:** match it to an option with `(Recommended)` ignored, and quote a label without it.
- **The pipe exits non-zero** (the exit is `open-gate.sh`'s, so read the stderr): a `render-spec-gate.sh:` line that isn't `install:` is a refused input; fix what it names, as step 3 or 4 sets, and run `procedure-approval-gate`'s step 1 again, once. A second refusal, an `install:` line or no renderer line: show the human the stderr, and end the spec talk with `stopped: spec not settled`, since no spec is settled without its gates.
- **The gate can't be shown or asked otherwise** (a failed `--question` or stdout that isn't one JSON object with those three fields, or `procedure-approval-gate` won't load or reports the plan not shown): tell the human what failed, word for word, and end the spec talk the same way.

1. **Settled already, or not needed:**
   - **On Resume with an open spec talk** (SKILL.md's Resume): a newer draft goes to step 2, then step 4's bullet for a left draft; an unanswered approval goes to step 4's approval ask.
   - **The approved spec is in the latest version (before v1: among v1's entries):** settled (step 6). After an ended grill, a ticket read after approval, or a settled answer from SKILL.md step 4's weighing, that changed its goal, done-when or scope, or when a flow runs this with such a change: step 2, then the revision offer (step 3), never forced. The human's own ask to revise it: step 2, then step 4 as a revision, with no offer.
   - **A recorded skip in the latest version (before v1: among v1's entries):** settled (step 6), unless the human now asks for a spec (step 2, then step 4), or a change altered the work's goal, done-when or scope (step 2, then a new spec's offer, step 3).
   - **The work now asked for builds nothing** (a grill about something else, such as the human's understanding of an existing system): no spec and no skip note; settled (step 6). Judge the work now asked for, never an earlier grill's.
   - **Otherwise:** step 2.
2. **Load `standard-spec`** with the Skill tool: it sets what a spec is and when one is needed. Missing: tell the human to install it, and stop (`stopped: standard-spec not installed`). Then step 4 only for a spec or revision the human asked for themselves, since their own ask needs no offer; step 3 for an offer step 1 set, a flow's change included. Otherwise, run step 4's `find` first: a draft left with neither outcome recorded means the human already chose to draft, so step 4, unless an offer made after that draft was answered "Keep talking"; then, or with no draft, step 3.
3. **Offer the spec at its gate, after the talk, never at once:** before v1, only once you judge the work is understood, at once on the human's own "no spec" (step 5), or on Resume for an offer gate left unanswered. The plan file is `{"case": "offer", "reasons": [...]}`, or `"case": "revision"` for a revised spec, written with the Write tool, its reasons from your judgment in the shape `render-spec-gate.sh --help` sets; after the human's "no spec", a reason cites their words. When a spec looks like overkill, a reason says why: the skip is the human's option. The label is `spec-offer`. A refused input: fix the reasons it names. Act on the answer as step 5 sets.
4. **Draft it** with the human. Run `sh <crucible.sh> find --effort '<effort folder>' --kind drafts --label spec`; its first line is the newest spec draft.
   - **A draft is left, with neither a recorded skip nor the approved spec** (a talk a break cut off): Read it in full and go on from it, never from memory; a complete one goes to the approval ask as it is.
   - **The latest version holds a recorded skip or the approved spec, and the newest draft is not the approved spec's own path:** it may be from a talk the human ended. Give the human its absolute path and ask whether to go on from it or start fresh; a reply that is neither is answered, and the question asked again.
   - **Each version of the draft,** the first and every revision during the talk, is a new file, since a draft is written once: run `sh <crucible.sh> new --kind drafts --effort '<effort folder>' --label spec`, Read the empty file it prints, then Write the draft into it; the newest is the current draft.
   - **A non-zero exit from either command, or a failed Read or Write of the draft:** show the human its error, and stop (`stopped: spec draft not created`).
   - **The approval ask,** after every draft, never in talk: write the plan file with `sh "${CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR:-$HOME/.claude}/skills/procedure-context/scripts/render-spec-gate.sh" --approval-plan --spec '<the newest draft's absolute path>' --plan-out '<the plan file>'`, the plan file being the path `procedure-approval-gate`'s step 1.1 prints, run anew for every showing, since a closed gate's folder is gone; a non-zero exit of it is read by its stderr as the pipe's is. The label is `spec-approval`. The view shows the draft's Goal, open points and path, from the draft itself, so never repeat them in the chat. A refused input: "the draft changed since its plan" means run `--approval-plan` again; any other draft problem it names means write a new draft and ask again. Act on the answer as step 5 sets.
5. **The human's answer at a spec gate.** Only the answer to the gate question this spec talk last asked writes the approved entry or the skip note: after any other answer, the same draft, or the skip, needs its gate shown again, since the gate's record proves only that it was asked. "No spec", "just build it" and "stop the work", typed at any gate, follow their own rules below, never a typed-answer rule.
   - **"Draft it now" or "Revise it":** load `standard-spec` as step 2 sets, when not yet loaded, then step 4.
   - **"Keep talking":** no draft. Before v1, back to discovery (SKILL.md step 1), offering again only after more talk; after v1, nothing is recorded: answer any question as the turn's last message, and show the offer gate again at the human's next message.
   - **"Skip the spec: say why":** drafting ends; record the skip note, quoting the words the human gave for it. Picked with none, ask why in plain chat as the turn's last message, and quote their reply; an option's label never becomes the note. At a new spec's offer after a recorded skip, a skip with new words records them as the skip note and retires the old one, with a note saying why. Then step 6.
   - **"Keep it as it is":** nothing is recorded (step 6).
   - **A typed answer at the offer gate:** one that asks to draft is the human's own ask ("Draft it now"); one that asks to skip is the skip, its words the note; any other: answer it, then act as on "Keep talking".
   - **A typed answer at the revision gate:** one that asks to revise is "Revise it"; one that declines is "Keep it as it is"; any other: answer it as the turn's last message, and show the revision gate again at the human's next message.
   - **"Answer the open points":** the human answers in the chat; write a new draft in which each answer moves into the section it settles and its point leaves `## Open points`, then step 4's approval ask.
   - **"Approve" or "Approve, leave them to the developer":** record the newest draft as the approved spec's entry with the note `Spec approved: "<the label>"`; the second accepts every open point still listed as the developer's call. A typed answer that approves the spec as it is counts as the approve option the gate showed ("Approve, leave them to the developer" when open points are listed), the note quoting its words. A revision also retires the old spec entry and its approval note; a spec after a skip retires the skip note; each retirement with its note saying why. Then step 6.
   - **"Change it: say what", or any other typed answer at the approval gate:** write the change into a new draft, then step 4's approval ask. "Change it" picked with no words: ask what in plain chat as the turn's last message first.
   - **The human's own "no spec" or "just build it", at any point** (discovery included): load `standard-spec` as step 2 sets, when not yet loaded, and open the offer gate at once (step 3), a draft left or not; in a revision, drafting ends with nothing recorded (step 6).
   - **`context.sh` exits 3 with `NO-SPEC-GATE`** when the outcome is written: show that gate again, the approval for the spec entry or the offer for the skip; never retype the entry or work around it.
   - **"Stop the work":** the drafts, and any context version, stay in the effort; stop (`stopped: spec not settled`). A later run goes on from the newest draft (step 4).
6. **Settled:**
   - **Run during discovery** (the human's own ask or "no spec" before SKILL.md step 4): keep the outcome for v1, and go back to SKILL.md step 1, then steps 2–4; v1 is never written early.
   - **Run from SKILL.md step 4:** go back to it, which writes v1 with the outcome.
   - **Run from SKILL.md step 7 after a grill:** go back to it, which asks "what next?".
   - **Run alone after v1 on the human's own ask:** once its extension is written, SKILL.md step 7 asks "what next?" again. With nothing recorded, tell the human in one line that the context stays as it was, hand a running flow the found version as SKILL.md step 8 sets, and end the turn with no "what next?".
   - **Run alone by a flow, or after a ticket read after v1:** hand over as SKILL.md step 8 sets, with no "what next?": the found version when nothing was recorded; with neither outcome in it, `stopped: spec not settled`.
   - **Run from Resume:** before v1, as Run during discovery; after v1, back to SKILL.md's Resume with the spec talk settled for this session, so only step 7's check is left.
   - Every stop here ends as SKILL.md step 8 sets, its step named `references/spec.md` step `<n>`.
