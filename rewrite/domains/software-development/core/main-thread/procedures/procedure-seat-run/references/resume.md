# Resume: the effort's seats after a break

Its terms and steps are SKILL.md's. Run when the caller's Resume loads this skill, once the caller's context is back, in one of two forms:

- **The whole effort** (no seat named): steps 1–3. A `tester` seat (its `role`), whatever its status: hand it back to the caller untouched, and run nothing for it.
- **Take up seat S** (the caller names one seat, with the inputs of its role, SKILL.md Interface): step 2 for S alone, its status and worktree from `sh "<seat scripts>/seats/seats.sh" get --effort '<effort folder>' --seat '<S>'`, its part as step 1 sets; read and take up no other seat; then hand S back with its record and what step 2 returns.

1. **The seats,** read only through the scripts: never run `events.sh` or read `events/`, but for an event file a script hands over by path (`iteration.sh`'s `LAST_REPORT:`). Run `list=$(sh "<seat scripts>/seats/seats.sh" list --effort '<effort folder>') && printf '%s\n' "$list" | jq -r '.seats[] | [.seat, .status, .worktree, .role] | @tsv'`. No seats: none is held (none was chosen, or every one was dropped): hand back to the caller with no seat held; its Resume says where it goes on. A seat's part is its task, from this session's todo item (`<seat>: …`) or the conversation (no script reads a dispatch's task); when neither holds it, step 2 says what to do. Never run `git` in a worktree or the checkout to learn a part; ask the human instead. When a read fails, show its output and record `not checked` for each seat it leaves unknown.
2. **One state per seat,** by its status:
   - **`chosen`:** a plan never answered: hand the seat back to the caller.
   - **`open`:** never launched (a denial, or a break before the call): hand its part back to the caller's plan step again when step 1 found its task; otherwise show the line `part_not_done`, with `--arg task '<seat>'` and `--arg why 'task unknown'`, and ask the human for its task; on an answer, hand it back to the caller's plan step with that seat; otherwise leave it.
   - **`working`:** its todo item records `not committed`, or no send since its last result: SKILL.md Results' `working` line, here the line `run_not_committed` shown with `--arg seat '<seat>'` and `--arg worktree '<worktree>'`. Otherwise, after a compaction in the same session, its run may still end: wait for it (SKILL.md Results). In a new session, or when you can't tell, ask the human in one AskUserQuestion whether its agent may still run, options in this order: `It may still run (Recommended)` → record it `not checked`, and relaunch nothing; `Its run has ended` → that same line (`run_not_committed`), its run ended. A typed answer: ask again, and relaunch nothing until an option is picked.
   - **`idle`:** run `sh "<seat scripts>/seats/iteration.sh" --effort '<effort folder>' --seat '<seat>' --checked '<task>'`, `<task>` the seat's name when step 1 found none: its line `checked_earlier` says done, planned (a tester's plan) or blocked, done and planned being SKILL.md Terms' seat done; a non-zero exit: show its stderr, and record `not checked`. Then hand the seat back with one of these, by its `LAST_REPORT:` file name (a gate left open across the break is asked again):
     - **ending `-test-plan.made.json`:** `planned`, or `plan-blocked` when `LAST_REPORT_STATE:` is `blocked` (a plan with a blocker); the caller settles its plan.
     - **ending `-build.committed.json` or `-test.committed.json`:** the label of the gate asked after it, `build-next` or `test-next`; run `sh "<seat scripts>/seats/gate-answered.sh" --effort '<effort folder>' --seat '<seat>' --label '<that label>'` and hand back that label with its `ANSWERED:` and `ANSWER:` lines, whatever they say: the caller shows the card and asks that gate again when it is unanswered, or when its answer is one the caller reopens. A non-zero exit: show its stderr, and record `not checked`.
     - **`LAST_REPORT: none`:** nothing; its card only when the human asks.
   - **`gave-up`:** SKILL.md Results' `gave-up` line, here the line `report_gave_up` shown with `--arg seat '<seat>'` and `--arg worktree '<worktree>'`.
3. **Then** hand back to the caller, with each seat's record.
