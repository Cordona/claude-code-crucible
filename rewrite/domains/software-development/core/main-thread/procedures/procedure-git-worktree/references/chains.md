# Chains

Read after a seat's new commit (`flow-code-development` step 4, `idle`) when a seat of the effort has `base_seats`, and when `mount-status.sh` gives a non-empty `parents_moved`.

1. **Check:** `list=$(sh "<scripts>/seats/seats.sh" list --effort '<effort folder>') && printf '%s\n' "$list" | jq -r '.seats[] | select(.status != "chosen" and ((.base_seats // []) | length) > 0) | [.seat, .status] | @tsv'`, the seat that just committed included; per seat, `sh "<scripts>/merge/chain.sh" --effort '<effort folder>' --seat '<seat>'`. `moved: false`: nothing to do. A read that exits non-zero failed: show its output, and offer nothing.
2. **Offer:** for a moved seat that is `idle`, ask with AskUserQuestion, header `Sync`, question `<seat> was cut from <the seats in parents whose in_seat is false>, which have newer work. Merge it into <seat>?`: `Sync now` (Recommended: its developer builds on the latest work) → step 3; `Not now` (nothing changes; offered again at the next commit) → nothing. A typed answer: ask again. A moved seat that is `working`: offer once its result is shown. A moved seat that is `open` or `gave-up`: offer nothing; the next check offers it once it is `idle`.
3. **Sync:** `sh "<scripts>/merge/chain-sync.sh" --effort '<effort folder>' --seat '<seat>'`. `synced: false`: nothing moved, say nothing. `synced: true`: show its `line` as a fixed line, and write its `message` into the seat's todo item: `flow-code-development` sends it first in the next change to that seat. Then run `sh "<scripts>/mount/mount-status.sh" --repo '<repo>'`, `<repo>` the seat's `repo` that `sh "<scripts>/seats/seats.sh" get --effort '<effort folder>' --seat '<seat>'` prints; a read that exits non-zero: show its output, and offer no remount. When its `seats` holds that seat, ask with AskUserQuestion, header `Remount`, question `<seat> is mounted in <repo> without its synced work. Remount it?`: `Remount` (Recommended: your checkout shows the latest work) → `sh "<scripts>/mount/unmount.sh" --repo '<repo>'`, then `sh "<scripts>/mount/mount.sh" --effort '<effort folder>'` with one `--seat '<seat>'` per seat in `mount-status.sh`'s `seats`, each `line` shown as a fixed line; `Leave it` (the mount stays as it is) → nothing.
